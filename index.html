02 / THE LETTER

What I wanted to say

I’m not expecting this letter to fix anything. I just wanted to say this properly.

I’m sorry for the way I handled things. I’ve thought about it since, and there are things I wish I had said differently and things I should have done better. I don’t want to make excuses for them.

I understand that an apology doesn’t mean someone has to forgive me or even respond. I just didn’t want to leave these words unsaid.

I can’t change what happened, but I can own my part in it. I’m sorry.
